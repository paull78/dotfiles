# Product Requirements Document Generator

Create a comprehensive PRD for: $ARGUMENTS

## Process:
1. **Discovery Phase**:
   - Ask clarifying questions about the product
   - Understand target users and market
   - Identify key stakeholders

2. **Document Structure**:
   - Executive Summary
   - Product Vision & Strategy
   - User Research & Personas
   - Feature Requirements (MoSCoW prioritization)
   - Technical Requirements
   - Success Metrics & Analytics
   - Go-to-Market Considerations
   - Resource Requirements
   - Timeline & Roadmap

3. **Deliverables**:
   - Main PRD document (markdown)
   - Requirements traceability matrix
   - User story backlog
   - Acceptance criteria checklist

Use tables, diagrams (mermaid), and clear formatting. Include sections for stakeholder review and approval.
